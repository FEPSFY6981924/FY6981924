In order to ensure safety in spaceflight, and by extension conducting experiments in space, we must work to understand and mitigate these risks. Examples include mental health issues (Inamdar, 2025), illness {}, muscle atrophy (Science Insights Team, 2026), and injury (Matza, 2026). These risks are especially concerning on long duration missions {} such as those on the International Space Station (ISS) {}.
